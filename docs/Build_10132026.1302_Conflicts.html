Conflicts and safeguards

EXISTING SAFEGUARD · PANEL TRANSFERS

Individual panel moves can change a scene camera track’s meaning

Individual moves into or out of a scene with camera keys remain blocked. Deleting a panel within a keyed scene also remains blocked. Clear the scene keys before editing its panel membership, or reorder the entire scene, which preserves its camera track. The application shows an explanatory message. These safeguards were present before this milestone.

RESOLVED · TIMELINE DURATION

Timing changes keep camera keys aligned

Stretching or squashing a panel scales its internal camera key times and shifts downstream keys by the duration change. Whole-frame rounding can cause collisions when a panel is made very short. Such edits are rejected atomically, and the user can move/delete a key first. Adding a panel at the end holds the final camera pose; duplicating artwork shifts downstream camera keys without copying the camera track.

RESOLVED · HOLD BOUNDARY

Hold interpolation switches at the exact next key

A model bug at intermediate keys was fixed: the held pose applies up to the frame before the next key, and that key’s authored pose applies on its exact frame. The regression is covered by a model test.

RESOLVED · NON-DESTRUCTIVE DRAWING

Camera movement never transforms source artwork

Drawing canvas coordinates and layer contents remain unchanged. Drawing and Panel thumbnails show IN/OUT overlays; Timeline and Pitch use the camera transform for playback. PNG export offers clean artwork, current camera framing, or IN/OUT overlays.

Scope and validation

The milestone implements 2D scene camera keyframes with linear, ease in/out and hold interpolation, numeric edits, direct pan/zoom handles, retiming, undo/redo and persistence. Rotation uses numeric controls; key time editing uses scene frame numbers and inspector controls. Audio tracks, 3D depth and PDF templates remain later milestones.
