Resolved behavior

STALE TEXT RECTANGLES

Old layout dimensions persisted after content changes

The previous bounds calculation included the stored text rectangle alongside rendered glyph measurements. That preserved extra width/height after shortening or restyling text. Bounds now derive from current content, including alignment, tracking, multiline layout, size, scaling and rotation. Existing text needs no recreation.

SECONDARY TEXT EDITING OPTION

Text can be edited directly on the stage

With Text selected, double-click Vector text to open an on-canvas editor with its caret at the end. Clicking outside or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter commits; Escape cancels; Enter adds a line. Completed edits participate in undo and save. Inspector editing remains available. Bitmap text stays flattened into pixels as in the previous build.

PLAYBACK PLACEMENT

Drawing and Timeline controls share the marked stage toolbar

Start/previous-frame/play-pause/stop/next-frame controls sit between breadcrumbs and view controls. Drawing plays the project with camera framing and returns to drawing on Stop. It introduces no play indicator in the panel strip. Timeline’s original playhead, scrubbing, camera lane and clip timing remain intact.

Validation and existing limits

Production build, 27 model/filesystem tests and six browser workflows pass. New checks cover caret placement, text commit/cancel/undo, shrinking and expanding text bounds, Drawing playback crossing panel boundaries, common control placement, and Timeline’s unchanged playhead. Layouts are checked at 1500×1000 and 1100×740.

Schema 2 compatibility, Bitmap editing/fill limits, and camera-keyed panel safeguards remain unchanged. Native Mac installer and physical tablet-pressure testing remain outstanding.
