Onion Skinning milestone · October 14, 2026 · Working branch

Delivered

  • Drawing toolbar checkbox between playback and zoom controls.
  • Independent Back and Forward counts from 0 to 3. Defaults: off, one in each direction. Settings are stored on this device.
  • Red backward skins and green forward skins. Nearer panels are stronger; farther panels fade.
  • Neighbors stop at the scene boundary. Visible Vector and Bitmap layers participate; locked visible layers remain referenceable.
  • Guides follow artwork coordinates and the Drawing view pan/zoom/rotation. They are not transformed by individual neighbor cameras.
  • Guides cannot be selected, painted, picked, or filled. They are excluded from project artwork, thumbnails, playback, and PNG exports.

Behavior considerations

No project-format migration or timing conflict was introduced. White areas are suppressed in skin rendering, including white paper baked into imported/flattened images. Very pale artwork produces weaker skins. Opaque artwork on the active panel can cover skins because guides are beneath active artwork. These rules do not change the original artwork.

At compact window widths the scene/panel breadcrumb is hidden to fit the requested controls; selection and metadata remain available in the panel strip and inspector. Other workspace toolbars retain their previous layout.

Validation

Production build, 28 model/filesystem tests, all six existing browser workflows, and the new onion workflow pass. The new workflow checks color/distance, independent counts, scene boundaries, hidden layers, Bitmap paper suppression, unchanged save data, playback exclusion, device persistence, and toolbar fit at 1100×740. Visual checks cover compact and full-size Drawing layouts.

Native Mac tablet and installer validation remain pending. Transitions, icon redesign, and Audio/Animatics have not started.
