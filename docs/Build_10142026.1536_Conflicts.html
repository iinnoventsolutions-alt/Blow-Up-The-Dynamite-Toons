How to use

  1. Select a panel with a following panel and at least two frames.
  2. Click + Transition. An existing transition at the same boundary is selected rather than duplicated.
  3. Click its marker to edit type, duration or direction. Duration respects the time/frames preference.
  4. Click Preview transition to seek to its first frame; press Play to watch it.
  5. Use − Transition or Delete transition in properties to remove it. Undo restores it.

Timing and boundary rules

SituationBehavior
Transition timingOccupies the outgoing panel’s final N frames; total runtime and panel durations do not change. Progress runs from 0 to 1 across those frames.
Camera motionOutgoing camera continues at the current frame. Incoming artwork uses its first camera pose during the transition, then resumes normal playback at its panel start. Scene camera settings remain independent.
Duration editAt least two frames, capped by the outgoing panel duration. Default is one second or the full outgoing panel if shorter. Invalid property input is rejected.
Panel shorteningTransition duration is clamped to fit. Shortening to one frame removes it. A notice explains the adjustment; Undo restores the original state.
Moves, duplication, deletionA transition stays with its exact adjacent pair. If an edit separates that pair, the transition is removed with a notice. Undo restores ordering and transitions together. No transition is automatically reassigned to new artwork.
Whole-scene reorderInternal transitions survive; broken cross-scene transitions are removed under the same adjacency rule. Existing camera safeguards remain.
Artwork outputsDrawing artwork, onion guides, panel thumbnails and single-panel PNG exports retain their existing behavior. Transitions affect playback. PDF/video export integration awaits those milestones.

Compatibility conflict

Project schema increases to 3. Schema 1/2 files migrate in memory and their source files remain unchanged until saved. Earlier builds reject schema 3 files instead of silently dropping transition data. Keep original copies for rollback testing. The human application version remains Version 1.
